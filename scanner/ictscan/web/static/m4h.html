A manipulation candle takes the previous 4H candle's high (low) and closes below (above) the previous candle's body. Entries: E1 at the 4H close · E2 limit at 50 % · E3 limit at a 1H/30m imbalance inside the candle · E4 the same pattern on 30m inside the candle (its own stop) · E5 limit at 70.5 %. Stop beyond the candle's extreme. Targets: 2R, the nearest untaken 4H/D1 swing (only if ≥ 2R), the candle's far end (limits only). Limits expire after three 4H candles.
